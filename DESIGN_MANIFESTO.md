# DESIGN MANIFESTO: The Architecture of OASIS

*Industrial Design Principles for the Algorithmic Oasis*

---

## 1. The Core Principle: Heat is the Program

An Atmospheric Water Generator (AWG) based on compression is, thermodynamically speaking, a heater that produces a small amount of water. Between 3 to 5 kW of heat is rejected as hot air for every few liters per hour produced. Any design concept that treats this heat rejection as a mere nuisance to be hidden behind a closed canopy or a decorative pavilion is fundamentally flawed.

**OASIS inverts this.** The thermal rejection becomes the organizing resource. It dictates the physical form, orientation, and cross-section of the object. The heat is recovered to regenerate the solar sorption bench, creating a physically linked **Value Cascade**. 

Consequently, OASIS is not a closed volume; **it is a gradient**. It provides a habitable shaded zone to the North, an open machine to the South, and a free-flowing air path from cool to hot, ensuring the AWG never suffocates on its own recirculated dry exhaust.

## 2. Form Typology: The Barjeel

The design draws inspiration from the *barjeel* (traditional Gulf wind tower)—an object whose climatic function directly produces its form. 

OASIS applies this principle in reverse: a high, shaded air intake at the North, a massive duct leading down through the sorption bed and the evaporator, and a high, unobstructed exhaust at the South. The separation of intake and exhaust is structural, preventing the recirculation of the 50°C exhaust plume. **The thermal constraint directly generates the silhouette.**

## 3. Honest UI: Revealing the Cost of Water

A machine producing water in an arid environment makes a political statement. Masking its energy cost behind decorative LEDs tells a false story of "free water from thin air."

OASIS displays its cost physically and honestly. The primary user interface consists of:
> **Two physical water columns side-by-side, at the same scale.**
> - **Left Column:** Water produced by the AWG machine (Low volume, High energy cost).
> - **Right Column:** Water recovered from the building's HVAC Condensate (High volume, Near-zero energy cost).

A graduated scale indicates the energy expended by each. The comparison is immediate, physical, untranslatable, and communicates the single most important lesson: AWG is justified only where passive alternatives do not exist.

## 4. Legible Mechanics

There is no hidden magic. The mechanics are legible at three distances:
- **At 30 meters:** The silhouette (the *barjeel*) and the height of the two water columns.
- **At 3 meters:** The circuits. Exposed plumbing with functional color-coding and manually operable zone valves. A gardener must understand the system without a manual.
- **At 30 centimeters:** Bilingual engraved maintenance tags (Arabic/English). Maintenance is the primary cause of failure in 50°C dusty environments; designing for it is central to the project.

## 5. Material Honesty & The Consumable Layer

Exterior materials in Abu Dhabi face extreme UV, 90°C surface temperatures, and dust. Claiming that plywood or cotton are "ecological" when they will disintegrate in 18 months is false. OASIS makes tenable material choices, embracing a replaceable "consumable layer":

| Function | Material | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Structure | Anodized Aluminum | Infinitely recyclable, local supply chain, UV resistant. |
| Thermal Mass & Ducts | Stabilized Earth | Useful thermal inertia, local sourcing, low embodied energy. |
| Shading & Filters | Date Palm Fronds (*Areesh*) | Abundant local resource. **An explicit consumable, replaced annually.** |
| Reservoir | Unglazed Terracotta | Passive evaporative cooling (5–10 K below ambient). |
| Sorption Glazing | Tempered Glass | The only transparent material that withstands 90°C under intense UV. |
| Contact Surfaces | Thermally Treated Wood | Tactile comfort, used exclusively in fully shaded areas. |

## 6. Functional Computer Vision

Computer vision is not used for redundant plant stress detection (which the load cells already measure). Instead, the cameras serve three unique functions:
1. **Biomass Yield:** A zenithal camera segments canopy cover daily, calculating the ultimate metric: *liters of water per square centimeter of canopy gained*.
2. **Condensation Kinetics:** A macro camera observes droplet coalescence on a test plate to study real-world nucleation in dusty, coastal environments.
3. **Dust Accumulation:** Photometric tracking of dust on the glass and solar panels to trigger maintenance, replacing inaccurate predictive models.

*Rule:* Cameras face the plants and the machine, **never the people**. All processing is done on the edge; no images are saved.

## 7. The Garden as the Interface

The garden itself is an extension of the UI. Divided into zones with a gradient of water requirements (from sensitive ornamentals to rugged local halophytes), the AI's rationing decisions become spatially legible. During water scarcity, one can see exactly which zones the OASIS system has chosen to sacrifice to save the core. Drought becomes information.
