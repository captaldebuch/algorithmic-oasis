# Research Outcomes & Academic Impact
**Project:** Algorithmic Oasis — SUAD AI Research Garden (2026–2028)  
**Affiliations:** SAFIR Lab · SCAI · Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi  
**Partners:** Ma Hawa AWG Technology

---

## Executive Summary

The **Algorithmic Oasis** pioneers **Eco-Autonomous Intelligence** in one of the world's most demanding climates. By coupling agentic AI with atmospheric water generation and solar energy management — and by deliberately structuring research around interdisciplinary student teams — this project produces outcomes relevant to three domains: sustainable AI, arid-environment engineering, and cross-domain research methodology. All outputs will be published open-access and the core software stack released as an open-source library.

---

## Research Vision

To establish **Eco-Autonomous Intelligence** as a recognized research field — investigating how agentic AI systems can optimize the metabolic cycles of biological life using non-traditional water sources (Atmospheric Water Generation) and intermittent energy (Solar), while generating evidence for how interdisciplinary student teams accelerate innovation in complex, real-world AI deployments.

---

## Research Pillar A — Autonomous Resource Management (AI & IoT)

**Core Outcome:** The **Predictive Thirst (PT) Algorithm**

A Reinforcement Learning (RL) framework that learns to balance plant biological needs against the energy cost of water production in extreme heat. Unlike threshold-based irrigation systems, the PT algorithm models the full causal chain: solar forecast → battery state → water production cost → soil moisture trajectory → plant stress index → irrigation decision.

**Scientific Contribution:**  
- First RL-based irrigation model trained and evaluated under hyper-arid coastal conditions (Abu Dhabi, UAE)  
- Validated against 12+ months of live sensor data from APKLVSR capacitive soil sensors and Ma Hawa AWG telemetry  
- Novel energy-biological trade-off function quantifying the marginal water production cost per unit of plant evapotranspiration

**Publication Targets:**  
- *IEEE Internet of Things Journal* (primary)  
- *Journal of Cleaner Production* (secondary)

## Research Pillar B — Arid-Environment Sustainability (AWG Integration)

**Core Outcome:** AWG Performance Atlas for the UAE Coastal Desert

The first longitudinal, data-driven performance mapping of an **Atmospheric Water Generator** (Ma Hawa Mobile Box) operating under variable solar irradiance, dust events, and seasonal humidity cycles in Abu Dhabi. This dataset addresses a critical gap in arid-climate water security literature.

**Scientific Contribution:**  
- Water yield versus energy cost curves under Abu Dhabi conditions (Oct–June season + summer extremes)  
- Modeled impact of shamal dust storms and high-salinity humidity on AWG intake and filtration efficiency  
- Open dataset released for replication and use by regional water-security researchers

**Publication Targets:**  
- *Energy and Buildings* (primary)  
- *Sustainable Cities and Society* (secondary)

## Research Pillar C — Interdisciplinary Research by Design (Pedagogy)

**Core Outcome:** The **Oasis Squad Model** — Evidence for Cross-Domain Team Performance in Applied AI

A methodological and pedagogical contribution documenting the outcomes of the Oasis Squad structure: deliberately mixed student teams (Computer Science, Physics, Arts) working collaboratively on a live, complex AI system. The research evaluates whether cross-domain team composition improves problem-solving robustness, reduces blind spots in system design, and produces more transferable competencies than single-discipline groups — specifically in the context of applied environmental AI.

**Scientific Contribution:**  
- Longitudinal study of student competency development across disciplines over two academic years  
- Formal comparison of cross-domain vs. single-domain team decision-making in hardware/AI integration tasks  
- Replicable team model and curriculum framework, contributed to SUAD's CS programme as a standing capstone  
- Open curriculum published for adoption by other universities in the Gulf region

**Publication Targets:**  
- *Journal of Engineering Education* (primary)  
- *Computers & Education* (secondary)
