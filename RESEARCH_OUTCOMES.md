# RESEARCH OUTCOMES & DISSEMINATION PLAN
**Project:** Algorithmic Oasis — SUAD AI Research Garden (2026–2028)  
**Goal:** Creating an AI-managed irrigation system that harvests water from the air.

---

## 1. Vision
The **Algorithmic Oasis** project builds a small-scale, autonomous irrigation system that captures water from the atmosphere. Managed by AI and maintained by students, it demonstrates a self-sufficient way to sustain green spaces in desert environments. The project aligns with SAFIR's commitment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 6, 9, 11) and Open Science.

## 2. Key Scholarly Pillars 

### Pillar A — Physical Lysimetry and The "Barjeel" Architecture (AI & Digital Policies)
**Core Outcome:** A validated physical evapotranspiration (ET) model, embedded within a structurally honest, thermodynamically driven physical prototype (the *barjeel* form factor).
- **Scientific Impact:** First continuous gravimetric mini-lysimeter dataset in the UAE. This ground truth trains an RL-based optimizer. The physical architecture demonstrates functional design, using materials like stabilized earth and aluminum to manage the 3-5 kWth rejected heat, while employing physical data visualization (e.g., dual water columns) instead of arbitrary digital screens to communicate water cost.

### Pillar B — AWG Performance Atlas & The Value Cascade (Environment & Sustainable Development)
**Core Outcome:** A formal "Data Paper" detailing a three-way comparative longitudinal performance mapping of AWG technology in the UAE.
- **Scientific Impact:** Publishes a highly-needed field dataset comparing three atmospheric water pathways (GEN-M1 Compression, CaCl₂ Solar Sorption, and HVAC Condensate) physically linked through a heat-recovery value cascade. The study will yield five definitive metrics:
  1. Real SEC vs. dew point over 12 months.
  2. Admissible AWG duty cycle.
  3. Fraction of irrigation met by passive HVAC condensate.
  4. The optimal dew point threshold for AWG activation.
  5. The true 10-year cost per m³ per pathway (positioning AWG against off-grid water trucks).

### Pillar C — The Oasis Squad Model (Mathematics & Pedagogy)
**Core Outcome:** Evidence for the efficacy of transdisciplinary "Agentic Workflows" in student training.
- **Scientific Impact:** A formal evaluation of how mixed-discipline teams navigate the hardware/AI integration challenges of environmental projects (SAFIR "Learning through Research" strategy).
