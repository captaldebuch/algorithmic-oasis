d. Research Council
The Research Council (RC) at Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi is responsible for promoting
and developing research, advising the Chancellor, Director of Research, and Head of SAFIR
on strategic research matters. Its mission includes fostering a supportive research
environment, identifying priority research themes, developing action plans for academic
excellence, reviewing policies and ensuring research integrity practices.
The RC shall consist of 10 faculty members designed through an election (serving for a twoyear term) and 3 ex-officio members (Chancellor, Director of Research and Head of SAFIR).
Among the 10 seats, 9 seats will be reserved for faculty professors and 1 seat for PhD
students & Postdoctoral researchers.

e. Members of SAFIR
SAFIR is the only research institute of SUAD. All professors at SUAD (assistant prof,
associate prof and full professors) are automatically affiliated at SAFIR as their primary
research affiliation. Lecturers who are holding a PhD are also affiliated to SAFIR if active
researchers. PhD students and postdocs who are benefiting from a research grant or PhD
students supervised by members of SAFIR will also be affiliate to SAFIR for the time of their
project.
For non-permanent faculty, the affiliation to SAFIR should be requested through an
application to SAFIR and the request will be reviewed and approved by the Research
Council. The details of the affiliation (responsibilities and engagements from both sides)
are given in the Affiliate Researchers Charter.

2. Internal Research Excellence Grant (IREG)
This grant serves as a key source of funding for research projects spanning one to three years,
with each project led by one or more of SUAD’s permanent or associate researchers in the
role of principal investigator.
These projects can be conducted either individually or as part of a collaborative team,
offering flexibility to accommodate diverse research approaches. The primary goal of SUAD's
funding is to support both the inception of new research initiatives and the continuation of
ongoing studies, ensuring that researchers have the necessary resources to pursue
innovative and impactful research.
Funding is intended to cover a wide range of operational expenses that are essential to the
successful execution of research projects. This includes, but is not limited to, the
procurement of materials, the organization and execution of fieldwork, the hiring of research
assistants, and other critical logistical needs. All the IREG projects are allowed to request up
to 80 hours of teaching reduction for the team for the whole duration of the project.
While SAFIR is committed to considering all proposals that meet its criteria, priority will be
given to projects that align with high-priority research themes identified by the institution.
Special consideration will also be given to interdisciplinary projects that bring together
expertise from multiple fields, as well as collaborative projects that involve partnerships
within and outside the university. In recognition of the importance of diversified funding
sources, project owners are encouraged to secure external funding from industry partners,
government agencies, or other academic institutions. This requirement not only broadens
the financial base of the project but also fosters valuable connections with external
stakeholders, enhancing the project's relevance and impact.
Finally, SUAD expects all funded projects to yield clear and tangible scientific deliverables.
These deliverables may include peer-reviewed publications, patents, or other significant
contributions to the scientific community.
The IREG will be open once a year and deadline and procedure should be respected to be
considered by the Research Council.

a. Faculty Research Sabbatical Leave
SAFIR supports and provides an opportunity for SUAD faculty to pursue research activities,
19

either in the UAE or abroad, while being relieved from teaching and administrative duties.
This opportunity is open to academic staff members—lecturers, senior lecturers, readers,
and professors—who hold permanent contracts. Sabbatical leave is not granted
automatically; it is awarded based on the merits of the proposal and the strategic needs of
the University.
Sabbatical leave typically lasts for six months, with two standard periods available: from 1
February to 31 July or from 1 August to 31 January. In exceptional cases, a sabbatical may
be extended to a full 12 months. Faculty on research sabbatical continue to receive their
full salary during this period.
Funding applications will be filled on SUAD E-Space under SAFIR

Each academic year, a limited number of six-month leave may be awarded. If no applications
meet the required quality standards, no sabbaticals will be granted.
Eligible research objectives for a sabbatical include:
► Initiating or finalizing a postdoctoral qualification, such as the HDR ("Accreditation
to Supervise Research")
► Advancing ongoing research projects, particularly those requiring travel abroad or
extensive fieldwork
► Writing a book or producing multiple peer-reviewed articles or chapters
Applications for a sabbatical must clearly outline the intended outcomes and justify the need
for dedicated time to achieve these goals. Sabbaticals will be assessed and awarded based

4. Doctoral and Postdoctoral Funds
Guided by its strategic research orientation and the needs identified by SUAD researchers,
the SAFIR is committed to supporting promising doctoral students and postdoctoral
researchers (PhD holders) each year. This support is aimed at fostering innovative research,
particularly in areas identified as high priority by the Research Council.
SAFIR will offer two types of funding opportunities, tailored to the research priorities and
needs of the institution:
► Fully funded positions for doctoral and postdoctoral researchers: These positions,
ranging from one to three years, are salaried and tied to specific research projects
identified by SAFIR. This funding is designed to provide comprehensive support for
researchers working on defined projects that align with SUAD’s strategic goals.
► Short research grants: These grants are intended to support fieldwork and other
research activities within the UAE, providing additional resources to early-career
researchers who require funding for specific aspects of their projects.

Funded PhD/Postdoc Research positions
The salaried positions offered can be funded either by the University or through external
funding sources, such as research collaborations between SUAD and industrial or
governmental entities. These positions are highly competitive, as they provide recipients
with the opportunity to earn a monthly stipend while completing their PhD degree.
Candidates are expected to complete their PhD within a three or four-year timeframe.
Candidates will benefit from a monthly stipend (15,000AED for PhD students and 18,000 for
21

Postdocs) and they will benefit from visa, health care, SUAD facilities, equipment, and
additional support for their research.

UAE Research Grant
The UAE Research Grant is specifically designed to support promising students in their
doctoral studies or postdoctoral careers who are conducting research in the UAE or GCC
region. While all disciplines are eligible, applicants must demonstrate a specific
institutional or research interest in the UAE and the broader GCC region. These grants are
intended to cover expenses associated with field research in the UAE, such as airfare, data
collection, travel to field sites, and accommodation at the SUAD residence. Additionally,
recipients will have access to office space and administrative support on the SUAD campus.
Eligibility to apply for UAE Research grants:
► PhD students who are in the research phase of their dissertations; a letter of
support from their PhD supervisor is required.
► Postdoctoral researchers who have earned their PhD within the past five years.
Grant recipients are expected to produce one research article as part of their funded
research and to deliver at least one presentation at the University.
This grant will be open once a year – date to be determined

5. Strategic Priority Research Grant
The Priority Research Grants aim to fund in-depth projects that will enhance SUAD’s research
profile and advance research and outreach on interdisciplinary, top-priority themes and
strategic subjects identified by SUAD research leadership. The grants will be open to
international researchers (not limited to SUAD) and aim at funding ambitious and
groundbreaking research on topics of national, regional, and global interest. The projects
should last between 2 to 5 years and a yearly financial and research plan will be submitted
by the team.
Priority funding shall cover, among others, the engagement of PhD students and postdocs,
as well as equipment and resources required for the cluster’s activities. In turn, a Research
Cluster commits itself to the creation of tangible deliverables, such as the number of projects
to be conducted in a set timeframe, the number of PhD graduates, the number of scientific
publications, the quantity of IP generated and protected, and an active webpage under the
SUAD website framework detailing the Cluster’s vision, structure, achievements and
activities.
This grant will be open once yearly or biyearly – date to be determined
