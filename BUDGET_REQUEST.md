# BUDGET PROPOSAL: Algorithmic Oasis (Pilot Phase)

**Portal:** [captaldebuch.github.io/algorithmic-oasis/](https://captaldebuch.github.io/algorithmic-oasis/)  
**Total Requested:** 21,300 AED  

---

The **Algorithmic Oasis** project requires specialized, industrial-grade hardware to transition from laboratory simulation to a high-fidelity "Living Lab" environment. This budget covers the acquisition of edge-computing nodes capable of on-site AI inference, high-precision sensing equipment for scientific data collection, and the necessary infrastructure for solar-powered water production.

## Compliance & Strategic Procurement
- **OASIS Alpha Kit (2,500 AED):** Immediate procurement. Replaces generic soil sensors with gravimetric mini-lysimeters (load cells) for high-fidelity scientific data collection in Month 1.
- **Deferred Capital Expenditure:** Heavy infrastructure (NVIDIA Jetson Cluster, LiFePO4 Power Buffer, and large irrigation suites) are strategically **deferred**. They will only be procured after the OASIS Alpha prototype physically measures the true water baseline (ET demand) and short-cycle AWG (50-70 L/day) parameters.
- **Compliance (Stream 4):** All individual equipment units are priced **below the 5,000 AED threshold**.
